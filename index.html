All 20 Korean Recipe Guides

Kimchi at Home

Making kimchi at home is simpler than most people think, and a single batch gives you a tangy, probiotic-rich side dish that keeps for weeks in the fridge. A longer read with extra tips and serving ideas.

Bibimbap

Bibimbap is Korea's most famous mixed rice bowl: warm rice topped with colourful vegetables, protein and a fried egg, finished with gochujang.

Bulgogi

Bulgogi is thinly sliced beef marinated in a sweet-savoury soy sauce, then cooked quickly over high heat until the edges caramelise.

Tteokbokki

Tteokbokki is Korea's favourite street snack: chewy rice cakes simmered in a spicy-sweet gochujang sauce. A longer read with extra tips and serving ideas.

Korean BBQ at Home

Korean barbecue at home turns dinner into an event: meat grilled at the table, wrapped in lettuce with rice, garlic and ssamjang.

Kimbap

Kimbap is Korea's portable seaweed rice roll, filled with seasoned vegetables, egg and protein, and sliced into neat bite-sized pieces.

Japchae

Japchae is a celebratory noodle dish of glass noodles tossed with vegetables and beef in a sweet soy-sesame sauce. A longer read with extra tips and serving ideas.

Sundubu Jjigae

Sundubu jjigae is a bubbling soft-tofu stew, spicy and comforting, served still simmering with an egg cracked in at the end.

Samgyeopsal

Samgyeopsal is thick-cut pork belly grilled plain at the table, then eaten wrapped in lettuce with garlic, chillies and ssamjang.

Korean Fried Chicken

Korean fried chicken is famously crisp: a thin, shattering crust, often glazed in sweet-spicy yangnyeom sauce or dusted with soy garlic. A longer read with extra tips and serving ideas.

Galbi

Galbi is marinated beef short ribs, grilled over high heat until charred at the edges and juicy inside, a centrepiece of Korean barbecue.

Doenjang Jjigae

Doenjang jjigae is Korea's everyday soybean-paste stew, earthy and savoury, packed with tofu, courgette, mushrooms and greens.

Naengmyeon

Naengmyeon is a cold noodle soup for hot days: chewy buckwheat noodles in icy beef broth with cucumber, pear and a boiled egg. A longer read with extra tips and serving ideas.

Mandu

Mandu are Korean dumplings, filled with pork, vegetables and glass noodles, then steamed, boiled or pan-fried until golden.

Korean Pancakes Pajeon

Pajeon is a savoury pancake loaded with spring onions and seafood, crisp at the edges and soft inside, served with a soy dipping sauce.

Kimchi Fried Rice

Kimchi fried rice is the best use for well-fermented kimchi: day-old rice fried with kimchi, gochujang and a fried egg on top. A longer read with extra tips and serving ideas.

Dakgalbi

Dakgalbi is a spicy stir-fried chicken dish from Chuncheon, cooked on a large flat pan with cabbage, sweet potato and rice cakes.

Jjajangmyeon

Jjajangmyeon is black-bean noodles: thick wheat noodles topped with a savoury chunjang sauce of pork, onion and courgette.

Korean Street Food

Korean street food is a whole world of snacks, from tteokbokki and fish cakes to hotteok and corn dogs, best eaten standing at a market stall. A longer read with extra tips and serving ideas.

Banchan Guide

Banchan are the small side dishes that surround every Korean meal, and learning a few of them transforms a simple dinner into a feast.
